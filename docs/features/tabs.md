# Repository tabs

- Every open repository has a tab in the bar at the top of the window. Click a tab to switch to it; the **×** on a tab (or a middle-click on it) closes it. Closing the active
  tab moves to its neighbour on the right, or the last one; closing the last tab returns to the start screen.
- The **+** at the end of the bar shows the start screen (recent repositories and your folder of repositories) to open another one in a new tab. Opening a
  repository that already has a tab just switches to it.
- Only the tab you are looking at is live. A background tab does **not** auto-fetch, watch its files or react to the keyboard. When you switch back, its graph and
  lists load again from the repository as it is now. The open diff, selected commit and terminal session of a tab are not kept while you are in another tab.
- The tabs are not remembered when you quit the app.
